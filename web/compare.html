Why it matters

  • Satellite says where to look. It's free, covers every farm every ~5 days, and that's what our map runs on.
  • Drone says what's wrong, plant by plant. At 10 m a whole orchard row blurs into one pixel. At 7 cm you can count trees, find the sick one, and spot weed patches between rows.
  • Precision treatment. A drone image lets a spray drone hit the affected rows instead of the whole field.
